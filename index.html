About

I graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Mapúa Malayan Colleges Mindanao. My experience is spread across a range of work — inventory and sales systems for small business owners, a chatbot, a game I built just to enjoy the process of building one, and applied AI projects.

The common thread has never been a particular language or toolset; it's the habit of checking whether a project actually works once other people use it. My most recent work leans on that hard, through rigorous testing and evaluation — but the same principle runs through everything I make. I'd rather deliver something simple and reliable than something clever I don't feel confident standing behind.
